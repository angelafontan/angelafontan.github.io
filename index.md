I am an Assistant Professor at the [Department of Decision and Control Systems](https://www.kth.se/is/dcs), School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS), KTH Royal Institute of Technology. I am also affiliated with [Digital Futures](https://www.digitalfutures.kth.se/). Since March 11, 2024, I have been a [WASP](https://wasp-sweden.org/) Fellow, funded by the Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation. My research interests are in the area of networked cyber-physical-human systems and nonlinear dynamics over networks, with applications to social networks, collective decision-making processes, and Live-in Laboratories.
{: style="text-align: justify;"}

I received a B.Sc. degree in Information Engineering in 2013 and a M.Sc. degree (with honor) in Automation Engineering in 2016, from the University of Padova, Italy. I received a Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering with specialization in Automatic Control in September 2021, from the [Division of Automatic Control](https://liu.se/en/organisation/liu/isy/rt), Department of Electrical Engineering, Linköping University, Sweden, where I was advised by [Prof. Claudio Altafini](https://users.isy.liu.se/en/rt/claal20/). From 2021 to 2024, I was a Postdoctoral researcher at the [Department of Decision and Control Systems](https://www.kth.se/is/dcs), School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS), KTH Royal Institute of Technology, working with [Prof. Karl Henrik Johansson](https://people.kth.se/~kallej/).
